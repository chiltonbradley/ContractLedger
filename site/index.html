A few things to know

Before the first upload.

Can I upload a contract today?

Not yet. This is our coming-soon page. Contract upload, OCR, and AI-assisted review are planned product capabilities. Early access timing will be shared when it’s ready.

Who is ContractLedger for?

People and teams managing agreements from either side: customers, vendors, service providers, and operators who want a clearer view of what they’ve agreed to.

Will it replace legal advice?

ContractLedger is intended to help you understand and organize agreements and prepare better questions. AI-assisted insights will support your review; they won’t replace a qualified legal professional.
